Every business has a ceiling it hasn’t reached and a ceiling it can’t.

The first is demand you aren’t capturing. The second is the month your list runs out, or your calendar does. Most forecasts model only the first, which is why they are wrong in month five rather than month one.

We start with what businesses like yours have done, then let your own numbers overrule us.

On day one the forecast is mostly our assumption, and the range is wide because it should be. Every real number you produce pushes ours further out of the way. You can watch it happen: the interval narrows, and the assumption’s share of the answer falls.

the maths, 40 seconds

Each rate in the funnel starts as a prior fitted at three levels — every engagement, businesses like yours, then you. The prior enters as pseudo-observations: evidence with a stated weight, not an opinion. Every real number you produce is combined with it by conjugate update, and the prior’s share of the answer — λ on the rail — falls as your own data accumulates. Nothing is overwritten; it is outweighed.

what this can’t know, 30 seconds

It can’t know whether your offer converts until it has watched it try. It can’t see a competitor undercutting you next quarter, a key hire leaving, or a regulator changing the rules. It treats your market as it behaved in the window the priors were fitted on. Where your niche isn’t one of the ones we hold priors for, it borrows from the global level and widens the range to say so. Everything it can’t know shows up as width, not as silence.
