the maths, 40 seconds

Each rate in the funnel starts as a prior fitted at three levels: every engagement, businesses like yours, then you. The prior enters as pseudo-observations, evidence with a stated weight rather than an opinion. Every real number you produce is combined with it by conjugate update, and the prior’s share of the answer, λ on the rail, falls as your own data accumulates. Nothing is overwritten; it is outweighed.

from the demonstration run above: the global-level prior on the headline figure is worth about 45 observations and currently carries λ 1.00 of the answer.

what this can’t know, 30 seconds

It can’t know whether your offer converts until it has watched it try. It can’t see a competitor undercutting you next quarter, a key hire leaving, or a regulator changing the rules. It treats your market as it behaved in the window the priors were fitted on. Where your niche isn’t one of the ones we hold priors for, it borrows from the global level and widens the range to say so. Everything it can’t know shows up as width, not as silence.
